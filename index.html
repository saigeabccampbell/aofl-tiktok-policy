Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Organic Views Metrics ("the App"), operated by Age of Learning, Inc. ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use our internal TikTok analytics tool.

Information we access

The App accesses the following data from TikTok via the TikTok API, solely on behalf of authorized Age of Learning employees:

  • TikTok account profile information (username, display name)
  • Video/post metadata (titles, publish dates, post IDs)
  • Organic performance metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, saves)

How we use information

Data accessed through the TikTok API is used exclusively for internal analytics and reporting purposes by Age of Learning's social media team. We do not sell, share, or distribute this data to any third parties.

Data storage

The App does not persistently store TikTok data on external servers. Metrics are fetched in real time and displayed within the App session only.

Data retention

We do not retain TikTok user data beyond the active session. No personal data is stored in databases or logs.

Third-party services

The App integrates with the TikTok API. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Security

Access to the App is restricted to authorized Age of Learning employees. OAuth access tokens are used to authenticate API requests and are not stored permanently.
